## What is Maggnn

Maggnn AI is an **all-in-one Chinese metaphysics + AI consultation platform**.

We do one thing well: the rigorous part of fortune-telling — **precise chart casting** — is handled by time-tested traditional algorithms, while **interpretation and insight** is handled by large language models. Open the site anytime, enter your birth details, and get an answer that is both professionally grounded and genuinely readable.

No jargon required. Enter your birth information — Maggnn does the rest.

- **Bazi (Four Pillars)** — full chart with Ten Gods, hidden stems, five-element strength, and 10-year luck cycles, with true solar time correction
- **Zi Wei Dou Shu** — 12 palaces, 14 major stars, four transformations, and triangle/square aspects
- **Plum Blossom Numerology** — time- or number-based hexagram casting with complete transformation analysis
- **Chinese Almanac** — solar-lunar calendar conversion, heavenly stems and earthly branches, daily guidance, auspicious hours
- **Fortune Trends** — life trajectory visualized as intuitive curves, so you can see your luck cycles at a glance

- **Three modes**: quick answers / deep thinking / expert-level reading — switch on demand

- **Free guest quota**: try it before signing up
